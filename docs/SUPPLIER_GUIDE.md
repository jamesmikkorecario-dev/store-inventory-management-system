# Store Inventory Management System (SIMS) - Supplier Portal Guide

Welcome to the SIMS External Supplier Portal. This interface provides third-party vendors and supply chain partners with real-time, transparent access to their assigned product catalogs, incoming purchase orders, and historical performance metrics.

---

## Accessing the Portal

1. Navigate to the portal login URL provided by your organization administrator.
2. Log in using your assigned email credentials (e.g., `supplier@sims.com`).
3. Upon authentication, you will be directed to your tenant-scoped **Vendor Dashboard**.

> **Security Note**: Your account is strictly bound to your organization's Supplier ID. You will only see products, orders, and metrics directly linked to your vendor profile.

---

## Portal Modules & Navigation

### 1. Vendor Dashboard (`/dashboard`)
Your operational command center displaying:
- **Total Catalog SKUs**: Count of active and inactive products assigned to your company.
- **Inventory Valuation**: Total monetary value of your stock currently stored in client warehouses.
- **Low Stock Alerts**: Highlighted items that have dropped below minimum buffer levels, indicating upcoming reorder demand.
- **Recent Shipments**: Log of recent stock-in delivery receipts.

---

### 2. Supplier Catalog (`/portal-catalog`)
View and monitor your complete product catalog:
- Search items by Name or SKU.
- Filter products by Category or Status (Active / Inactive).
- Review current warehouse stock levels and agreed unit cost pricing.

---

### 3. Purchase Order Tracking (`/portal-orders`)
Monitor all purchase orders generated by client warehouse staff for your company:
- **Order Statuses**:
  - `Submitted`: Order raised and awaiting administrative approval.
  - `Approved`: Order authorized—ready for fulfillment and dispatch.
  - `Received`: Goods delivered and verified at the warehouse.
  - `Cancelled`: Order terminated prior to shipment.
- **Order Details**: Click on any purchase order row to view itemized SKU quantities, unit costs, delivery timelines, and submission timestamps.

---

### 4. Performance Scorecard (`/portal-performance`)
SIMS tracks supplier reliability using automated metrics to ensure transparent partnership evaluation:

- **On-Time Fulfillment Rate (%)**: Evaluates whether shipment receipt timestamps occurred on or before the agreed **Expected Delivery Date**.
- **Defect & Damage Rate (%)**: Calculates the proportion of delivered items subsequently marked as damaged or rejected during receiving reconciliation.
- **Order Volume Tracking**: Historical tally of total orders fulfilled and monetary volume processed.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

- **Can I modify product prices or stock counts directly?**
  No. To maintain warehouse integrity, stock counts and pricing modifications must be processed through official purchase orders or warehouse staff transactions. Contact your procurement manager for catalog pricing adjustments.
- **What should I do if an Expected Delivery Date needs to be rescheduled?**
  Contact your warehouse administrator referencing the Purchase Order Number so they can update the order timeline in the management system.
